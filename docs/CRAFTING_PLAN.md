# Crafting plan

The five crafting lines are **built** (1 Nov 2026). The rules they follow are in `DESIGN_RULES.md` §4-5; the data is in
`tools/armory_data_v25.py` (Wolfpack, Moonhowl) and `tools/armory_data_v26.py` (everything else), talismans in
`tools/build_remastered.py`. This file tracks what exists and what could come next.

## Built

| Line | Pieces | Where the plans are |
|---|---|---|
| Hunter's leather | Wolfpack (mantle, jerkin, breeches, grips) -> Moonhowl (same + longbow) -> Skyhunter (mantle, grips, hood) | Crooked Otto -> Hagen's reward -> Tallon hunter |

| Talismans | wolf, Alpha's Fang, boar, rat, bear, Warding Charm I-II-III (replaced the crab, toad and carapace charms), ember, spider, tatzelwurm, scorpion (Murolosh), wyrm horn | Otto, Moorbridge druid, Tallon hunter, Murolosh |
| Iryan upgrade | Stormhowl Shoulder Guards (Nasreddin's shoulders + Stormhowl's heartstone) | Tallon hunter |

Materials that exist only in one place: Silver Ruff (White Fur), Moon-mad Pelt and Sinew, Greymaw's Mane, Stormhowl's Heartstone
(the mad pack in the Dark Forest), Dark Wolf Pelt (big wolves after the tutorial).

## Ideas for later (not built)

Ordered by how much they would add.

1. **Smith metals, chapter 2 step:** Kosh-steel plans in the Moorbridge smith that each need one beast part, so heavy-armour
   players also craft in chapter 2 (today the heavy line jumps from Ferdok steel to Tallon).
2. **Bosses drop crafting cores:** one unique part per story boss (like Greymaw's Mane) that upgrades a set piece to a named
   variant. Pairs well with the pending "tougher unique beasts" change.
3. **Rhulana and Gwendala lines:** their heirloom sets exist; a crafted alternative for each (Amazon sabre-and-shield leather,
   elven bow leather) would give the companions their own progression.
4. **Alchemy consumables tied to hunting:** a potion per beast family (toad wart antidote, ember-sac fire ward, louse-chitin
   magic ward) at the herbalists of each chapter.
5. **Trophy shield:** Forgrimm's two-hander leaves Rhulana as the only shield user; a crab-shell / scale shield line for her.
6. **Murolosh and later:** a fourth tier for Tusk-and-claw and Witch-cloth using dwarven steel and late beasts (scorpions, spiders),
   bought in Murolosh, so the lines don't stop at Tallon.
7. **Repair loop:** let dismantling an old set piece give back one beast part, so outgrown gear isn't wasted.
