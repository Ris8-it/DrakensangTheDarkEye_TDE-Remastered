# Secret quests

Part of **Drakensang TDE - Remastered** (installed by the pack's `Install.bat`, after armory_v2 and remastered_systems). New game required.
None of these quests are announced. Wendelin, the rumour-monger, points you to all of them.

## Wendelin Raunefuss, Teller of Rumours
Stands by the entrance of the Gentle Sow tavern in Wagoner's Home, Ferdok. His rumours are free and he has plenty:
- Fighters: leads to Master Klingenhand and the Crimson Circle.
- Magic: leads to Rakorium's codex.
- Outside Ferdok: Ulfried in Avestrue, and the chickens.
- Treasure: hints at the dragon hoard, the dwarven treasure, the Fire Falls and the Tallon crypt diaries.
- One rumour each for Rhulana, Gwendala and Forgrimm, shown only while they are in your party.

## The Crimson Circle (Ferdok, whole game)
**Start:** Master Alrik Klingenhand, a retired fencing master, sits in the Golden Lance tavern. Ask about his story and accept. You get **Klingenhand's Ledger**, which lists every duelist's hiding place.

Seven duelists, each a human with a legendary weapon they wield against you and drop when they die. Each also drops a **Crimson Sigil**, gold and crafting gems. Talk to a duelist and choose "Draw" to start the fight; "Not today" lets you come back later.

| # | Duelist | Where | Weapon (drops) | Companion moment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Jorgo Fairwind, the Harbour Riposte | Ferdok harbour, where Ardo fell | Harbour Riposte (rapier) | |
| 2 | Ilgrim Ashblade | Blood Mountains, ruined castle | Ashblade (Khunchom saber, fire) | |
| 3 | Faelwen Duskarrow, Hunter of Elves | Moorbridge marsh, the great tree (archer) | Duskarrow (elven bow) | Gwendala |
| 4 | Sir Wolfhart the Oathbreaker | Above the Silver Falls, among the dead | Oathbreaker (bastard sword) + Pavise of the Broken Oath | |
| 5 | Sabeth Lionsbane, the Outcast | Tallon, the tatzelwurm's cave | Lionsbane (Amazon saber) | Rhulana |
| 6 | Grimbart Ironbeard, the Exile | Deeps of Gruldur, the old teleporter | Exile's Cleaver (two-handed axe) | Forgrimm |
| 7 | Ravena Blutfeder, the Crimson Master | Behind the Fire Falls, the fire bridge | **Crimson Vow** (mythic dirk) | |

Difficulty rises from 120 LE (Jorgo) to 450 LE, AT 23, PA 19 (Ravena). They use feints, aimed thrusts and knockdowns.

**Rewards from Klingenhand:**
- **3 sigils:** Klingenhand's Fencing Gloves (AT +1, PA +1) and 80 ducats.
- **7 sigils:** **Ring of the Crimson Circle** (mythic: AT +1, PA +1, Agility +1, Courage +1, battle aura) and 100 ducats.
- **Quest XP:** 30 to 100 per duelist, plus 200 at the end.

**Rhulana and Sabeth:** once Sabeth is dead, with Rhulana in the party and Lionsbane plus her heirloom tooth in your packs, tell Klingenhand. Rhulana asks to lay the saber down in Sabeth's honour: Lionsbane is gone, and the tooth becomes the Mane of the Lioness (heirloom stage II) at once. Or keep the saber for its reforge.
Klingenhand also waits in **Murolosh** later, so the seven sigils can be handed in there.

## Pal'Na'Thar's tempering (behind the Fire Falls)
After he forges your Fire weapon, ask him "Can you temper what I carry?". He gives the plans for that weapon's tempered form (+1 damage, +1 Attack, one signature effect; costs the weapon, a Heavy Wyrm Scale and Dwarven Steel). One Fire weapon, one tempering: the best weapons in the game.

## Small moments
- The Blood Mountains woodcutter gives you Holdwin's Splitting Maul (a two-hander for Forgrimm) once Holdwin is home.
- The skeleton in the Moorbridge tree hollow (the elven ghost's lair) holds 5 Elf wood for Gwendala's bows and heirloom.

## The Mad Pack and The Moon-Thread (Dark Forest to Tallon)
**The Mad Pack:** fifteen moon-mad wolves led by Greymaw and the white storm-wolf Stormhowl roam the western woods of the Dark Forest.
Greymaw wears a witch's charm. Bring it to Knowall, the witches' talking toad: he finds a silver thread in it that no witch spun,
knots it for you (**Moon-Thread Knot**) and gives you 20 Moon-sinew Arrows. That starts **The Moon-Thread**:

| Step | Where | What happens | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|
| Not Witch-Work | Ferdok, Count's Town | Show the knot to the alchemist Auralia: moon-silver, spun from moonlight by the little folk | 2 healing potions |
| Empty Cages (optional) | Silver Falls, on the road to the castle | An abandoned trappers' camp, three escaped wolves led by Cage-Breaker, a torn bill of sale | pelts, sinew, 6 ducats |
| One Who Answers Straight | Tallon (after his riddle) or Ferdok (at Auralia's stall) | The leprechaun spun the thread and sold it to **Baltram Seyfried**, a collector of pelts | |
| The Moon Road | the leprechaun | He opens a moon road to the witches' dancing place in the Dark Forest | |
| The Collector | the dancing place | Baltram, two trappers and five chained wolves (storm, ember, frost, rot, shadow), one by each skull pillar; each dead wolf puts its skull out | 30 ducats, gems |
| The Moon-Mother | the dancing place | When all are down she rises from the fire: she howls in wolves (75 %), the moon goes dark (50 %), she tears at the last chain (25 %); she cannot die | |
| Thread or Pelt | the leprechaun, by the fire | **Cut the thread:** she runs free, **Moon-Mother's Tear** (MR +2, LE +8, AU +8, IN +1, Wilderness Lore +2). **Take her pelt:** **Moonmother Mantle** (Moonhowl Mantle +1 armour on chest and back; Sneak +3, MR +2, AT +1, ST +1, AU +10). Either way: the six Moonhowl plans Baltram stole from the witches | |

Afterwards the leprechaun takes you back to Tallon (next to his meadow), down into the forest, or leaves you with the skulls.
Ulfried in Tallon knows Baltram, and Knowall has something to say about your choice.

## The Codex of Hidden Fire (Rakorium, Ferdok house, whole game)
Ask Rakorium in your Ferdok house "Is there anything I could do for your research?". The question appears in every one of his greetings, whichever chapter you are in. He wants the four pages of the Codex Pyrdaconis:

| Page | Where | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| I: Of Embers | Ferdok sewers | 2 Scrolls of Haste and 2 Scrolls of Mending |
| II: Of Ashes | Blood Mountains, ruined castle chest | Wand of Rime |
| III: Of Bones | Moorbridge crypt, sarcophagus chest | Rakorium's Spare Spectacles (Cleverness +1, Intuition +1, MR +1, Arcane Lore +3) |
| IV: Of Dreams | Castle Grimmzahn vaults | 2 Scrolls of Battle Trance and 50 ducats |

Bring each page as you find it. With all four, and once Japhgur is dead (only a dragon's death lets him read "Of Dreams"), ask him what happens next. He reads the codex aloud, which summons an **Echo of the Codex** (260 LE, MR 14) right in your house. He hands you his **Robe of Disputation** (legendary cloth robe: AE +10, MR +2, Cleverness +1) and his **Best Hat** (legendary; the Second-Best Hat is elsewhere in the pack) and supervises from behind a chair. Rakorium cannot die.

## Notes
- Duelists are placed only in areas you can return to. Nothing is on Drakensang Mountain.
- Quest items (sigils, ledger, codex pages) cannot be sold, stolen or destroyed, and are taken when you hand them in.
- Untested in game so far: the archer duelist's bow use, and the Echo's spawn inside the house. If the Echo does not appear, the rewards are still given.
- Remove with the pack's `Uninstall.bat`.
