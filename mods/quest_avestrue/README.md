# Ulfried Pelzfeld, trophy collector

Ulfried travels with the hunters: Avestrue (by Owlstone), Ferdok (Praios Square, by the bowyer), Tallon (by the hunter) and Murolosh (by the trader). All four share one quest line. Handed-in trophies are kept by him (they do not reappear in his shop).

## Teeth for the Collector
Bring 2 Wolf Fangs (skinning, or White Fur, who always drops 2 plus his own great fang). Reward: Wolf-fang Necklace, 10 ducats, 15 XP.

## Beasts of Legend
Five one-off elite beasts with unique trophies:

| Beast | Where | Talisman |
|---|---|---|
| Old Grimtusk | 20 | Grimtusk Charm: LE +3, Willpower +2 |
| Ashpelt the Elder | 35 | Ashpelt's Talisman: AU +8, LE +4 |
| Queen of the Marsh | 40 | Marsh Pearl Ring: Magic resistance +2; Immune to poison |
| The Ember Queen | 45 | Ember Crown Pendant: AE +8; Immune to burning |
| The Broodmother | 60 | Broodmother's Heart: Agility +2, Dexterity +1, Dodge +1; Immune to poison |

All five: **Heart of the Wild** (LE +10, AU +10, Courage +1, Dodge +1, Magic resistance +1) and 50 ducats.
