# Heirloom sets (spoiler-light)

Each party member has a set: **4 unique pieces** hunted across the whole game, and an **heirloom** that grows in 4 stages.

| Who | Set | Heirloom (slot) | Armour type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rhulana | Regalia of the Lion Queen | Lion's Tooth of Kurkum (amulet) | metal (heavy fighter) |
| Forgrimm | Ferolax's Forge-Panoply | Ferolax's Hammer-Ring (ring) | metal (heavy), plated boots 8 |
| Gwendala | Moonwarden's Raiment | Seed of the Moon-Tree (amulet) | leather (no spell penalty) |
| Arcane Thief | The Stolen Grimoire | Grimoire Locket (amulet) | leather/cloth (no spell penalty) |

**How it works**
- Stage I of each heirloom is found in Ferdok or Moorbridge, somewhere that matters to that character.
- Stage II recipes are sold in Tallon, stages III and IV in Murolosh. Stage IV needs a Heavy Wyrm Scale from Japhgur's hoard (there are 14 now).
  Crafting: Rhulana's and Forgrimm's at a forge (Smithing), Gwendala's at a bow bench (Bowcraft), the Grimoire at an alchemy bench (Alchemy; the Arcane Thief no longer has the thief penalty).
- From stage II the heirloom gives a bonus **for every set piece the party owns**, and from stage III an extra bonus for owning all four. After finding a new piece, take the heirloom off and put it back on.
- **Reforges** (Murolosh, endgame): a recipe turns a legendary weapon you found into its final mythic form, with a Heavy Wyrm Scale. Options: Fang of the Lioness or Lionsbane (Rhulana), Last Oath or Exile's Cleaver (Forgrimm), Bow of Sala Mandra or Duskarrow (Gwendala), Crimson Vow or Phex's Last Laugh (Arcane Thief). Use whatever you find until then; the reforge takes it one step further.
- Shoulders: Lioness Pauldrons (Rhulana, from the outcast Amazon), Moonwarden's Mantle (Gwendala), Thief's Patch-Mantle (Arcane Thief).
- Gwendala also gets more bows (Moonbow, Hawkstring, Thornwood Warbow, Silverleaf Longbow), an archer's glove and three arrow types.
